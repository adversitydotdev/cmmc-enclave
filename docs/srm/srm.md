## Legend

Responsibility is from the company's point of view (OGRL RFC 0001, the company is the author).

| Cell | OGRL responsibility | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Inherited (AWS) / (Google) | `subservice` | The vendor carries it; the company does nothing beyond choosing the service |
| Shared | `shared` | The company configures its part; the vendor carries the rest |
| Customer | `provider` | The company does it |
| — | none | No implementation for this component |

A cell shows the strongest company burden across the mapped controls (Customer, then Shared, then Inherited). A suffix (planned) or (partial) means at least one contributing implementation is not yet `implemented`. Status is the rollup across all components: implemented (all implemented), planned (all planned), partial (otherwise). Customer remains is yes when any component is Customer.

Customer buckets that always remain: policies, people, physical sites, endpoints.
