## Purpose

Keep unauthorized people away from CUI in offices, equipment rooms, and home offices.

## Scope

Every site where CUI is processed or stored: {{ sites }}, including equipment rooms, CUI media storage areas, and approved alternate work sites. Cloud data centers are covered by the providers.

## Policy statements

1. A list of people authorized to enter each site is kept, including any home office approved for CUI. Keys and badges are issued only after approval. The list is reviewed quarterly. Access is removed within 1 business day of termination. (3.10.1)
2. Each office or equipment room uses badge readers or keyed locks. Entry logs are kept at least 1 year. Unissued keys are locked up, access devices are inventoried annually, and locks are rekeyed on loss. (3.10.1, 3.10.2, 3.10.5)
3. Visitors sign in with name, organization, host, and times, and are escorted in non-public areas. Visitor records are kept at least 1 year and reviewed monthly. Anomalies go to the security team. (3.10.3, 3.10.4)
4. Entry points are monitored by video or badge-system alerts. The access log is reviewed monthly and after alarms. Suspected physical incidents follow the Incident Response Policy. (3.10.2)
5. Equipment rooms with in-scope gear keep temperature and humidity within manufacturer ranges with alerting, have a UPS for orderly shutdown, fire detection, and known water shutoff. Cloud-only sites mark this not applicable in the SSP. (3.10.2)
6. **Alternate work sites.** Home offices and other alternate sites are approved in writing by the security lead. Public spaces (cafes, airports, coworking common areas) are not approved for CUI. Each worker acknowledges the safeguards: managed encrypted device only, approved remote access only, screen locked and devices and media in a locked room or container when unattended, screen and conversations kept from household and public view, no printing of CUI unless authorized. (3.10.6)
7. Compliance at alternate sites is assessed annually by self-attestation, and endpoint compliance reports are reviewed. Issues are followed up. (3.10.6)

## Roles

- **Facilities owner (or IT lead):** keeps lists, logs, and devices.
- **Security lead:** approves alternate sites and reviews attestations.
- **Remote workers:** follow statement 6 and attest yearly.

## Exceptions

Written approval from {{ ssp_signer_name }}, naming the site, safeguards, and end date.
