## Purpose

Make sure everyone who touches CUI knows the risks, the rules, and how to report problems.

## Scope

All managers, employees, and contractors of {{ company_name }}, with extra duties for administrators, security staff, incident responders, and anyone with privileged or CUI-handling duties.

## Policy statements

1. Everyone completes security awareness training within 30 days of start and annually after that. Training covers CUI handling and marking, phishing and social engineering, incident reporting, and the company policies. (3.2.1)
2. Phishing simulations run at least quarterly. (3.2.1)
3. Training content is reviewed annually and updated after significant incidents, policy changes, or new threats. (3.2.1)
4. System administrators, security staff, incident responders, and anyone with privileged or CUI-handling duties complete role-based training before they get access, and again annually. Role content is reviewed annually and after major incidents or technology changes. (3.2.2)
5. Insider threat training for all managers, employees, and contractors happens within 30 days of start and annually. It covers the documented indicators (for example unexplained access to information outside job duties, or bullying of coworkers) and the reporting channel. (3.2.3)
6. Completion is recorded per person with date. The role roster is compared with completion records each year. Records are kept at least three years. (3.2.1, 3.2.2, 3.2.3)
7. Access is not granted, or is suspended, for people who miss the training deadline until they complete it. (3.2.1, 3.2.2)

## Roles

- **Security lead:** owns content, schedule, roster, and records.
- **Managers:** make sure their staff finish on time.
- **Workforce:** complete training and report suspicious activity to the security team's reporting address.

## Exceptions

Written approval from {{ ssp_signer_name }}, with a make-up date no more than 30 days out.
