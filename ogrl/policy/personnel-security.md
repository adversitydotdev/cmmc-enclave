## Purpose

Make sure people with access to CUI are vetted before they get it and lose it promptly when they leave or change roles.

## Scope

Employees, contractors, and interns of {{ company_name }}.

## Policy statements

1. Before any account on a CUI system (Identity Center permission set or CUI Workspace account) is granted, identity verification, criminal history, and employment history checks are complete, to the extent law allows. Rescreening happens on transfer into a privileged or high-risk role. (3.9.1)
2. HR tells IT of every termination and role change the same day. Termination triggers the Offboarding Procedure. (3.9.2)
3. On termination, access is disabled within 24 hours, keys, tokens, and credentials are revoked, devices and CUI are recovered, and an exit briefing covers continuing confidentiality duties. (3.9.2)
4. On internal transfer, access is reviewed within 5 business days. Access no longer needed is removed. (3.9.2)
5. CUI protections continue after a person leaves: confidentiality agreements stay in force and former personnel get no access to CUI. (3.9.2)
6. Checklists and screening completion dates are kept so they can be compared with account creation and termination dates. (3.9.1, 3.9.2)

## Roles

- **HR (or the manager if there is no HR):** runs screening, notifies IT of events.
- **IT lead:** executes disablement and revocation.
- **Security lead:** verifies completion and recovers CUI.

## Exceptions

Written approval from {{ ssp_signer_name }}. Access before screening completes is never excepted for privileged roles.
